# Abstract

This study examined the relationship between working memory (WM) and inhibitory control in a sample of 48 participants; the experiment was completed in 2019, and two raters independently coded the data and checked their agreement, yielding a complete dataset for analysis [3].

The results show that working memory capacity strongly predicted inhibitory control performance (r = 0.42, p < 0.01). This result is theoretically important.

We propose that activation in the prefrontal cortex may moderate this relationship, but the small sample does not support causal inference.

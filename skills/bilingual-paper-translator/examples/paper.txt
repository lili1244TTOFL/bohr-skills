Sleep restriction impairs working memory in 60 young adults [1]. We did not observe a recovery after 2 days of sleep restriction (Fig. 2). The effect size was 0.42 (Smith et al., 2018), and 15% of participants showed no decline.

Sleep restriction was applied for 7 days with a 5-hour window. Working memory was measured by the n-back task with a load of 2 and 3 items.
